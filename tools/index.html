Wander Tools

Free tools for developers who test location features. They run in your browser, need no account, and do not upload the routes or files you give them.

Simulator GPX Builder and Fixer

Build a route file that Xcode's iOS Simulator and the Android Emulator will actually play, at a speed you choose. Or drop in a GPX, KML, GeoJSON or CSV file and see what stops it working: track tags Xcode ignores, missing or repeated times, fractional seconds, swapped latitude and longitude.

Developers

Coordinate Converter

Paste a location in any common format and get the others: decimal degrees, degrees-minutes-seconds, degrees and decimal minutes, Plus Codes and geohash. It also gives you links that open the spot in Apple Maps, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap.

Everyone

Looking for step-by-step guides instead? The Wander blog covers Xcode's Simulate Location, Android mock locations and geofence testing.
