The difference that matters

We never modify
the game.

Tools like PGSharp, iPogo and the old Global++ apps work by handing you a rebuilt copy of the game itself. Wander doesn't touch the game at all — it changes what your phone reports, and the real, unmodified app reads that like any other location.

What Wander does

  • Changes the location your device reports, at the system level — over Apple's own developer tunnel on iPhone, and the built-in mock-location developer setting on Android.
  • Leaves you on the real app you installed from the App Store or Play Store, updating normally, signed by whoever made it.
  • Everything on the phone reads that same system location — maps, weather, your games. There's nothing game-specific about it.

What Wander never does

  • Never patches, repacks or re-signs an app you didn't get from the store.
  • Never injects code into a running app or hooks its internals.
  • Never touches your game account or a game's servers — no game sign-in, no bot, no automated tapping, no reading data out of a game.

Straight with you: this is not a promise that you can't be banned. Changing your location can break an app's terms all on its own, and moving in ways a human couldn't is detectable behaviour no matter how the location got there. What we can say precisely is what Wander doesn't do. Niantic sued Global++ over its modified Pokémon GO, Ingress and Wizards Unite clients; in January 2021 Global++ agreed to a $5 million settlement and a permanent injunction. Features

Everything you need to
be anywhere.

All the power of pricier tools like iMyFone AnyTo — on iPhone, Android, Mac & Windows, with a real free trial. Pick your transport mode, fly a real flight path, set a schedule, or download maps for offline use.

One-tap Teleport

Drop a pin anywhere on the map — a landmark across the world or the coffee shop down the street — and you're there instantly. Search by name, paste coordinates, or tap the map.

Live Joystick Walk

Move your location in real time with an on-screen joystick — walk, run, bike or drive speed. Push to steer, farther to go faster.

Multi-stop Routes

Chain several stops into one trip and pick how you travel — drive, walk, cycle or public transport. Wander snaps to roads, paces realistic speed, humanizes the motion, and can auto-loop.

Flight Planner

Pick two airports or a time range, then fly the great-circle route between them — off a bundled airport dataset, no API. Boat mode sails open water the same way.

Geofence & Scheduling

Set Wander to be at a place during set hours, and let geofence triggers hand you back to real GPS the moment you actually arrive somewhere. On iPhone & Android.

Offline Maps

Download a map region over Wi-Fi, then pan, zoom and teleport with no connection — OpenStreetMap tiles cached on device. On iPhone & Android.

PoGo Mode

Jump-teleport with a cooldown timer and block-until-cooldown lock — plus presets for Pokémon GO, Monster Hunter Now, Pikmin Bloom & Ingress.

Share a Spot as a Link

Send a spot — or a whole multi-stop route — as one link, with no export file to pass around. On the other end they copy the link, paste it into Wander on iPhone or Android, and get a preview before anything moves.

Hands-free Walking

Point the joystick once and tap Lock heading — Wander keeps walking that way without you holding anything. Set a distance goal and it stops itself on the number, not past it.

Several iPhones at Once Beta

The Mac and Windows apps hold a location across every connected iPhone — set one, set a few, or broadcast to all of them. Connect each phone first, then pick who moves. Handy when a whole household needs to be in the same place.
