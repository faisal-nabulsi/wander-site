Refund Policy

Simple: a full refund of any payment within 60 days, no questions asked.

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Wander is an indie app, and we'd rather have a happy user than a grudging refund holdout. This policy explains how refunds work for every paid plan: monthly, yearly and the one-time "lifetime" purchase. It works alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Who processes payments

All Wander purchases go through Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record for our sales. That means Lemon Squeezy handles the checkout, the payment, taxes, and the payment records. Wander never sees your card number. Because Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record, refunds are ultimately issued through them — but you can just contact us and we'll take care of it with them on your behalf.

Any paid plan — a full refund within 60 days

You can get a full refund of any payment within 60 days of that payment, no questions asked. This covers every paid plan: monthly, yearly and the one-time "lifetime" Pro. For a subscription, each payment gets its own 60 days.

You don't need to give a reason. If something isn't working, we're happy to help fix it, but that's your choice, not a condition of the refund.

Subscriptions — cancel any time

The subscription option can be cancelled at any time. When you cancel:

  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for, and
  • You won't be charged again — no future renewals.

Cancelling stops future charges. Any subscription payment made in the last 60 days can also be refunded in full, as described above.

How to request a refund

Just reach out — whichever is easiest:

  • Email faisalnab25@gmail.com, or
  • Message us on Discord (discord.gg/gfHdsRXUVA).

Please include the email address on your account / purchase so we can find your order. We usually reply quickly. Once a refund is approved, Lemon Squeezy processes it back to your original payment method; how long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

A note on the free plan

Wander has a free plan so you can confirm it works on your device before you pay. We really recommend trying it first — that's the surest way to avoid needing a refund at all.

Changes to this policy

If we update how refunds work, we'll change this page and the effective date at the top. Any purchase is covered by the policy that was in effect when you bought, or by this one if it is better for you.
