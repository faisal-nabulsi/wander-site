How setup works

Neither app comes from the App Store. Both need a computer for the first iPhone install.

Wander

  • iPhone: you need a Mac or Windows PC once. The free Wander Installer signs in with your free Apple ID and installs the app over USB. This is sideloading, which means installing an app from outside the App Store.
  • On the phone, you trust the developer in Settings and turn on Developer Mode. Developer Mode is an iOS setting for testing apps. Apple notes it reduces some built-in protections.
  • You also install LocalDevVPN, a free tunnel app from the App Store. Setup takes about 10 minutes.
  • After that, Wander re-signs and updates itself over the air. Like iAnyGo, it must be refreshed inside the app within each 7 days. You do this in Wander’s Settings with LocalDevVPN connected. If 7 days pass without a refresh, you need the computer again. Why apps expire after 7 days.
  • Android: install the APK (the Android app file) from our site. No computer. It takes under a minute.
  • Mac and Windows: Wander Desktop drives a connected iPhone, with nothing installed on the phone. The desktop builds are unsigned, so macOS and Windows warn you before the first launch. The Windows iPhone installer is a beta.

Tenorshare iAnyGo

  • iPhone app: Tenorshare’s install guide has you install the iAnyGo iOS Assistant on a Windows PC or Mac. You log in with an Apple account and connect the iPhone by USB.
  • On iOS 17 and later you also turn on Developer Mode. Then you trust the app in Settings.
  • The guide also has you allow a VPN configuration through its IPLocate app, which the assistant installs.
  • The guide says the app’s validity must be reset every seven days. You do this inside the app by logging in with your Apple ID. No computer is needed for the reset.
  • Desktop app: you connect your iPhone or Android phone to the computer. Then you pick a place on the map.
  • Android: Tenorshare offers an Android app download on its product page.

Who should pick which

Pick Wander if…

  • You want the lowest price. Pro is $3.99 a month, $36 a year or $80 once.
  • You use more than one platform. One license covers iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, and follows your account.
  • You want to test for days before paying. The free plan refills every day and month.
  • You want to read the code. Wander’s source is public on GitHub.

Pick iAnyGo if…

  • You need more than 3 phones. A desktop license connects up to 5 iPhones, and Android phones without a limit.
  • You want support around the clock. Tenorshare advertises 24/7 technical support.
  • You want a quarterly plan. Wander only sells monthly, yearly and lifetime.
  • You want a money-back guarantee with a stated 30-day window.

Wander is an actively developed project. Read the install guide before you start.

Questions people ask

Is Wander cheaper than Tenorshare iAnyGo? ▾

Yes, on every monthly, yearly and lifetime plan we compared on 17 November 2026. Wander Pro is $3.99 a month, $36 a year or $80 once. The iAnyGo iOS app for one iPhone was $14.99 a month, $79.99 a year or $99.99 for a lifetime license, before tax.

Do I need a computer to set up Wander or iAnyGo on an iPhone? ▾

Yes, for both. Wander uses the free Wander Installer on a Mac or Windows PC. Tenorshare uses its iAnyGo iOS Assistant on a computer. Both apps are then signed with an Apple ID and must be refreshed inside the app about every 7 days. Tenorshare says you do this by logging in with an Apple ID inside the app. Wander does it with Run self-refresh in its Settings while LocalDevVPN is connected. If Wander goes 7 days without a refresh, you need the computer again. See why sideloaded apps expire after 7 days.

Does one license cover iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows? ▾

One Wander Pro license covers all four, on up to 3 devices. Tenorshare says its single-platform licenses are not cross-platform. Its bundle license covers Windows, Mac, iOS and Android.

Do Wander or iAnyGo need a jailbreak? ▾

No. Both run on a normal iPhone. Wander's setup turns on Developer Mode. Tenorshare's guide also turns on Developer Mode for iOS 17 and later.

The free plan refills, so you can test the whole setup before you pay. If it works for you, Pro unlocks iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows with one license.
