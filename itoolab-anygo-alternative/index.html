How setup works

On iOS 16 and later, both need a computer to get started.

Wander

  • iPhone: you need a Mac or Windows PC once. The free Wander Installer signs in with your free Apple ID and installs the app over USB. This is sideloading, which means installing an app from outside the App Store.
  • On the phone, you trust the developer in Settings and turn on Developer Mode. Developer Mode is an iOS setting for testing apps. Apple notes it reduces some built-in protections.
  • You also install LocalDevVPN, a free tunnel app from the App Store. Setup takes about 10 minutes.
  • After that, Wander re-signs and updates itself over the air. Like any app signed with a free Apple ID, it must refresh within each 7 days. You do this in Wander’s Settings with LocalDevVPN connected. If 7 days pass without a refresh, you need the computer again. Why apps expire after 7 days.
  • Android: install the APK (the Android app file) from our site. No computer. It takes under a minute.
  • Mac and Windows: Wander Desktop drives a connected iPhone, with nothing installed on the phone. The desktop builds are unsigned, so macOS and Windows warn you before the first launch. The Windows iPhone installer is a beta.

iToolab AnyGo

  • iPhone app: iToolab’s install guide runs on the iPhone. You download the app, then trust the developer in Settings, General, VPN & Device Management.
  • On iOS 18 and later the phone restarts. Then you install a profile from the guide page.
  • iToolab’s component page and technical FAQ say iPhones on iOS 16 and later also need the AnyGo desktop app on a computer. It turns on Developer Mode and installs the “AnyGo components”.
  • Desktop app: you choose a mode, then connect your iPhone or Android phone to the computer. iToolab also lists changing location over Wi-Fi.
  • Android: the app is listed on Google Play.
  • iToolab says its Windows software is code-signed and that it is a registered Apple developer for its Mac software.

Who should pick which

Pick Wander if…

  • You pay monthly or yearly. Pro is $3.99 a month or $36 a year.
  • You switch platforms. One license covers iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, and follows your account.
  • You want to test for days before paying. The free trial refills every day and month.
  • You want to read the code. Wander’s source is public on GitHub.

Pick AnyGo if…

  • You run many phones at once. A personal desktop license controls up to 15.
  • You prefer desktop software that its maker says is code-signed.
  • You want the Android app from Google Play, or a quarterly plan.

Wander is an actively developed project. Read the install guide before you start.

Questions people ask

Is Wander cheaper than iToolab AnyGo? ▾

For monthly and yearly plans, yes. On 17 November 2026 Wander Pro was $3.99 a month and $36 a year. AnyGo for Windows was $9.95 a month and $49.95 a year, and the AnyGo iOS app was $19.95 a month and $79.95 a year, before tax. For lifetime plans, AnyGo for Windows was listed at $79.95 and Wander at $80. iToolab's store also showed a 20% coupon (ANYGOPRO) and a 30%-off lifetime offer that day. With either one, AnyGo for Windows lifetime ($63.96 or $55.97) cost less than Wander. That license runs on one Windows PC (it controls up to 15 phones) and does not include Mac, the iOS app or Android.

Does AnyGo need a computer to install on an iPhone? ▾

On iOS 16 and later, yes. iToolab's trust guide for the AnyGo iOS app runs on the iPhone. But iToolab's component page and technical FAQ say iPhones on iOS 16 and later also need the AnyGo desktop app on a computer, to turn on Developer Mode and install the AnyGo components. Wander needs a Mac or Windows PC for the first iPhone install. After that it refreshes itself over the air, as long as it refreshes within each 7 days. See iToolab's component page and our post on why sideloaded apps expire after 7 days.

Does one license cover iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows? ▾

One Wander Pro license covers all four, on up to 3 devices. iToolab says its Windows and Mac licenses only work on their own system. Buying the AnyGo iOS app adds free Windows and Mac licenses as three separate registration codes. The Android app is sold separately.

How many devices can I use? ▾

Wander Pro works on up to 3 devices. iToolab says a personal AnyGo desktop license controls up to 15 devices. The AnyGo iOS app plan on its store supports 5 iPhones.

The free trial refills, so you can test the whole setup before you pay. If it works for you, Pro unlocks iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows with one license.
